🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 18, 2026

Welcome to Ultimate Publisher. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

📌 Quick Summary: We collect minimal data necessary to provide our services. We never sell your personal data. You have full control over your information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

We may collect the following personal information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and profile picture (when you sign in via Google or Facebook)
  • Social Media Account Data: Access tokens and basic account information from connected platforms (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok)
  • Content Data: Videos, images, captions, and metadata you create or upload for publishing

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: App usage patterns, features accessed, time spent in app
  • Performance Data: Crash reports, app performance metrics

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • ✅ Enable you to connect and manage your social media accounts
  • ✅ Create, schedule, and publish content to your social media platforms
  • ✅ Generate AI-powered content suggestions (using DeepSeek AI)
  • ✅ Provide analytics and insights about your content performance
  • ✅ Improve our app features and user experience
  • ✅ Send you important notifications about your scheduled posts
  • ✅ Respond to your support requests

3. Third-Party Services

We integrate with the following third-party services:

Service Purpose
Google (YouTube) YouTube channel connection and video publishing
Facebook Facebook Page connection and content publishing
Instagram Instagram Business account connection and posting
TikTok TikTok account connection and content publishing
DeepSeek AI AI-powered content generation (titles, descriptions, hashtags)
🔑 Important: We only store OAuth access tokens securely on your device. We never store your social media passwords.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Your data is stored locally on your device using encrypted SharedPreferences
  • Access tokens are stored securely and are never transmitted in plain text
  • We use industry-standard encryption protocols for data transmission
  • We do not store your data on external servers unless explicitly required for the service

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data only in the following cases:

  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly authorize us to share data
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights
  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party services that help us operate the app (subject to confidentiality agreements)

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • ✅ Access: View all data we have collected about you
  • ✅ Delete: Delete your account and all associated data at any time
  • ✅ Disconnect: Disconnect any social media account from the app
  • ✅ Opt-Out: Opt-out of analytics and crash reporting
  • ✅ Export: Export your data in a portable format

7. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy within the app
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Sending you a notification (if you have enabled notifications)
